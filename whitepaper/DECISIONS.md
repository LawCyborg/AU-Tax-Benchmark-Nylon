# Design decisions: Whitepaper

One line per decision that applies to this folder. The full justification, known cost and source for each is in the register, `../DECISIONS.md`, under the same id.

| \# | Decision | Justification | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| P1 | Headline correctness claim worded as "scored significantly higher on correctness than every other system", in the whitepaper | True of the mean Correctness score: Nylon's lead excludes zero against every system (closest, Fable: +0.26 \[+0.04, +0.47\]).  | Documented |
| P2 | Publish only the whitepaper without cost figures; the costed version is internal | Nylon's cost is commercially sensitive and cannot be included | Documented |
| P3 | No version history for this folder before publication | — | Documented |
| P4 | Whitepaper says Nylon's product advantages ("non-public, government-origin data", "single-interface verification of every source" and others) were "either not relevant to this benchmark or not used, so none contributed to Nylon's results" | Nylon's research library is not among those listed advantages: it is primarily public, government-origin data, and is part of the harness the benchmark set out to measure ("its mapping of legal data to the way practitioners find authority", whitepaper §1). Nylon's precedent system was turned off for the run | Documented |
| P5 | Encrypt the questions and reference answers (`questions/questions.jsonl`) and the systems' answers (`responses/`, and the candidate-answer section of `judge-inputs/`); key on request from benchmark@usenylon.com, given an agreement not to use it to compete with CPA Australia | The questions and answers are, or may contain, copyrighted CPA Program material. CPA Australia has been asked for permission to release the questions; if it is granted, the questions and answers will be decrypted | Documented |
| P6 | Whitepaper's "Other specialist tools" sentence kept without detail | The authors' preliminary examinations found no specialist research AI that outperformed GPT-6 Astra or Claude Fable 5.1; no further detail is given | Documented |
| P7 | Whitepaper Discussion says Nylon's answers most often earned a perfect correctness score | The authors treat a correct answer as one with a perfect Correctness score (5 out of 5). On that measure Nylon leads every system: 62% of its answers, against 40% for Claude Fable 5.1 and 2–18% for the others, with every paired 95% interval clear of zero (`scripts/checks.py` §1). The rubric was designed so that the total scores reflect each answer's value to a reviewing practitioner, not for any single lower Correctness level to be read as a separate measure of correctness. Nylon also gives an estimated certainty rating with each answer, which alerts users to likely inaccuracies | Documented |
